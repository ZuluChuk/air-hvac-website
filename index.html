How the guide rates work

These rates are intended for trade-only, pre-agreed, multi-week bookings — not one-off call-outs or an automatic price for every site. The minimum commitment, duration, location, duties and attendance arrangements are agreed before a booking is accepted. Specialist diagnostics, VRF/VRV commissioning, urgent attendance and out-of-hours work are quoted individually.

Agreed before the job

Working hours and door-to-door travel arrangements, mileage outside the M25 (normally 50p/mile by agreement), parking, tolls, materials, refrigerant, hired access equipment and any overtime are confirmed separately. Standard payment terms are seven days from invoice unless otherwise agreed.
