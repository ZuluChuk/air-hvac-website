Air conditioning repairs and fault finding

If your air conditioning is not cooling, showing an error code, leaking water or repeatedly stopping, AIR HVAC can investigate the cause and explain the practical repair options.

Request fault finding ↗
01

Common problems

Poor cooling or heating, frozen coils, condensate leaks, unusual noises, control faults and systems that stop unexpectedly can have several possible causes.

02

A diagnosis before replacing parts

Fault finding may involve checking controls, filters, airflow, drainage, electrical components and refrigeration performance as appropriate. Any parts or further work are discussed before proceeding.

03

Information that helps

When contacting us, include the make and model, error code if displayed, a description of the fault, postcode and photos of the indoor and outdoor equipment where practical.
