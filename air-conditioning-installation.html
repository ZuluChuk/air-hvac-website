Air conditioning installation

From a single bedroom unit to a multi-room or commercial system, AIR HVAC plans and installs air conditioning with attention to equipment selection, pipework routes, drainage and the finished appearance.

Systems for your space

Wall-mounted systems for bedrooms, lounges and offices; multi-split systems for several rooms; and ducted or cassette equipment where the building layout suits them.

02

Survey and installation planning

We consider indoor and outdoor unit locations, access for servicing, condensate drainage, pipework routes and electrical supply requirements before confirming the installation scope.

03

Testing and handover

Installations include appropriate refrigerant-circuit testing, evacuation, commissioning and customer handover in line with the equipment and work undertaken. Electrical supply arrangements are agreed in the quotation.
