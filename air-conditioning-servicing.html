Air conditioning servicing and maintenance

Routine servicing helps keep air conditioning equipment clean, reliable and working as intended. AIR HVAC supports domestic and commercial systems across Surrey and Greater London.

Enquire about servicing ↗
01

What a service can include

Depending on the equipment and access, maintenance may cover filters, coils, drainage, condensate pumps, airflow, operating checks and visible signs of leaks or damage.

02

Domestic and commercial systems

We work with wall-mounted units, ceiling cassettes, ducted systems and more complex commercial HVAC installations. The service scope is tailored to the equipment on site.

03

Maintenance frequency

The right interval depends on usage, environment and manufacturer guidance. Busy commercial premises or dusty environments may need more frequent attention than lightly used domestic systems.
